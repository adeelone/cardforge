# CardForge Organization Pilot

## Purpose

Run a low-risk business-card workshop or cohort rollout for students, founders, staff, or community members using one approved design system.

## Suggested Pilot

- Participants: 10-30 people.
- Owner: one staff member responsible for roster accuracy and print approval.
- Duration: one workshop plus one proof-review cycle.
- Data: only participant-approved public contact fields.
- Output: individual digital cards, one roster PDF, and optional JSON backups.

## Workflow

1. Choose a template and set brand colors, fonts, dimensions, bleed, and finish.
2. Download the CSV template from Team & roster in the editor.
3. Collect consented fields and import the completed roster locally.
4. Review each variant for spelling, overflow, contrast, and QR readability.
5. Configure sharing fields and expiry for the cohort.
6. Export the roster PDF and print one physical proof.
7. Correct issues, export the final PDF, and archive the approved CSV and CardForge JSON in organization-managed storage.
8. Delete the local CardForge workspace and working roster after the organization's approved retention period.

## Success Criteria

- Every participant approves the public fields on their card.
- No text clips or crosses the safe area.
- Every QR scans at final size on at least two current mobile devices.
- The print provider accepts the PDF dimensions, bleed, and crop marks.
- The organization can reproduce the output from its archived CSV and CardForge JSON.
- The owner records participant consent, retention period, storage location, and final disposal.

## Do Not Use the Static Edition For

- confidential directories;
- regulated personal data;
- identity credentials or access badges;
- automatic roster synchronization;
- centrally revocable digital identities;
- workflows requiring SSO, audit logs, or administrator-enforced permissions.
